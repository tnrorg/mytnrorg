### 🌐 TNR Digital Community Platform

A production-ready digital community and organization management platform
built to support membership, administration, verification, applications,
organizational workflows, and community services.

**Key Features:**
- Member registration and profile management
- Membership approval and verification workflows
- Role-based administration
- Council and organizational management
- Application and decision workflows
- Email notifications
- Member directories and location-based data
- Analytics and reporting
- Responsive user interface
- Database-driven operations
